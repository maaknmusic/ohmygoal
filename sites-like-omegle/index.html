The sites, one by one

  • OhMyGoal (this site): runs in the browser, free, with an on-device age estimate, nudity blur, a scam shield for phone numbers and UPI IDs, and the app in 9 Indian languages. Premium (₹49 for 7 days) adds gender and country filters and Live translate.
  • OmeTV: owned by Bad Kitty’s Dad, LDA in Portugal; website and apps with country selection and translation. Wikipedia notes the age check is a checkbox. OmeTV alternative
  • Monkey: an app and website for quick video chats with an 18+ rule; Avast and Bitdefender report there is no real age verification. Monkey alternative
  • Chatroulette: the original random video chat, launched on 16 November 2009 by Andrey Ternovskiy, then 17, in Moscow. Chatroulette alternative
  • Azar: an app by Hyperconnect, which Match Group bought in 2021; offers voice-to-subtitle translation and an “In-Match Blur”, with gender and region filters behind a subscription. Azar alternative

How to choose

  1. Prefer a service that checks age for real and blurs nudity before you see it.
  2. Look for one-tap Report and Block, and bans that stop the same person coming back.
  3. Be wary of anything that asks for money to “unlock” someone, or pushes you to move to WhatsApp or Telegram.
  4. Read our safety guide: the scams are the same on every site.
