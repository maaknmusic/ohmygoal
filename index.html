Stay safe

Strangers are strangers. A few habits keep chats fun:

  • Never share your phone number, UPI ID, OTPs, address or social handles. Scams start by moving you to another app.
  • Nobody needs money from you here. Don’t send any, and don’t click links from strangers.
  • Report and block in one tap (flag and ban icons). We review reports, and repeat offenders are banned by device and network.
  • Your video is never recorded and goes directly between you and your partner. Partner video stays blurred until our on-device check passes.
  • Asked for photos or threatened? Stop replying, block, and report. In India call 1930 (cybercrime helpline) or visit cybercrime.gov.in.

We never sell unbans or verification. Appeals are free.
