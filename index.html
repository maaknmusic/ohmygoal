FAQ

Straight answers.

Something missing? Contact us and a person will reply.

Is it really free?

Yes. Chatting, interests, games, reactions and every safety tool are free, and you don't need an account to chat. Friends need a Google sign-in. Premium is an optional pass for extras such as choosing who you meet by gender or country.

Is my video recorded?

No. Video goes peer-to-peer between your browser and your partner's, and we don't record or store it. If your network blocks a direct link, an encrypted relay may carry the stream so it can connect. It still isn't saved. Remember the other person's device could record, so don't share anything you wouldn't want out.

How does the age check work?

Before your first chat, your camera estimates your age on your device and asks for a small head turn so we know you're live. Camera frames are not uploaded or stored. If you look under 18 or can't be checked, you can't chat, and we may re-check during video calls.

What if someone is rude or unsafe?

Tap Report or Block in any chat. Reports go to moderators. Nudity is blurred on your device, and a scam shield warns you when someone shares a phone number, UPI ID or social handle. In India you can also call 1930 or visit cybercrime.gov.in.

Can I get banned, and can I appeal?

Yes, if you break the rules or fail an age or nudity check. Bans apply to the device, network and account, and the ban screen tells you why. Many restrictions end on their own after about 15 minutes or 24 hours. Appeals are free, and a ban can't be bought off.

How does Premium work? Can I get a refund?

Premium is a one-time UPI payment for 7, 30 or 90 days (₹49, ₹149 or ₹349). It doesn't auto-renew, so there's nothing to cancel. If you were charged but Premium didn't switch on, or you were charged twice, write to us within 7 days and we'll fix it or refund you. Unused passes can be refunded within 48 hours. See the refund policy.

What data do you keep?

Messages and video aren't stored. We keep short-lived matching records and abuse reports, and, to enforce bans, your IP address and a device hash. If you sign in with Google we also keep your Google ID, name, email, friends and Premium status. Read the full privacy policy.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. It works in a modern browser on Android, iPhone and desktop, and you can pin it to your home screen. Video needs a camera and a steady connection. Text chat is lighter if your data is low.

When do voice mode and rooms arrive?

We're working on both. They're marked “coming soon” until they're ready, and we won't promise dates we can't keep.

Stay safe

Strangers are strangers. A few habits keep chats fun:

  • Never share your phone number, UPI ID, OTPs, address or social handles. Scams start by moving you to another app.
  • Nobody needs money from you here. Don’t send any, and don’t click links from strangers.
  • Report and block in one tap (flag and ban icons). We review reports, and repeat offenders are banned by device and network.
  • The “✓ 18+” chip next to a name means that person’s device ran our on-device age check. It is an estimate, not ID verification, so it never replaces your own judgement.
  • Report a single message with the “Report” link under it. That message’s text is sent to moderators with your report. If someone sends several messages we flag, we’ll offer to skip or report them.
  • Your IP address can be seen by the person you chat with, as in any peer-to-peer call. Premium members can hide it (Menu → Hide my IP address).
  • Your video is never recorded. It is encrypted and goes only to your partner (directly, or through a relay that cannot read it). Partner video stays blurred until our on-device check passes.
  • Asked for photos or threatened? Stop replying, block, and report. In India call 1930 (cybercrime helpline) or visit cybercrime.gov.in.
