A short history

Andrey Ternovskiy, then a 17-year-old student in Moscow, launched Chatroulette on 16 November 2009. It grew to millions of users within months and is still running. Its early years also showed why moderation matters, and it brought in moderators and recognition software.

What OhMyGoal adds

  • Voice-only and text-only chats, not just video.
  • Interests and themed rooms (cricket, study, music, language exchange).
