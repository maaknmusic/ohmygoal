Group video chat with strangers

Sometimes one-to-one is too much pressure. Group rooms let you join a conversation already going: video rooms for up to 4 people and voice rooms for up to 8.

Start chatting →Free · no sign-up · 18+

  • By topicJoin a room about something you like.
  • Leave any timeNo pressure to stay.

How group rooms work

  1. Open Group rooms from the start page.
  2. Choose Video or Voice and a topic.
  3. You join a room with space, or a new one opens. Everyone connects directly to everyone else.

Good to know

  • Group rooms are in beta: tell us what you would like next.
  • The same rules apply as in one-to-one chats, and you can report anyone in the room.
  • For bigger audiences, Live sessions let a host talk to many people at once.

Questions

How many people can join a group video chat?

Video rooms hold up to 4 people and voice rooms up to 8.

Is group chat free?

Yes.

Can I report someone in a group?

Yes. Report and Block work in group rooms too.
