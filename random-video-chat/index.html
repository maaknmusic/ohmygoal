How random video chat works here

  1. Choose Video, Voice or Text. Add a few interests (cricket, music, study…) if you like, so you meet people who like the same things.
  2. Your camera estimates your age on your device before your first chat. Camera frames are never uploaded.
  3. We match you with a random person who is looking at the same moment. The call connects peer-to-peer.
  4. Talk, play a quick game, send a reaction. Press Next whenever you want someone new, or Report and Block if someone breaks the rules.

What makes it different from other random chat sites

  • Safety is built in, not sold. Blurring, the scam shield, reporting and blocking are free for everyone.
  • A scam shield. If someone shares a phone number, UPI ID or social handle, you get a warning, because that is how most scams start.
  • Games and icebreakers. Tic-tac-toe and other quick games and conversation starters so you are never stuck at “hi”.

  • 18 languages for the whole interface, including Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali and Marathi.

Works on your phone

It runs in any modern browser on Android, iPhone and desktop. You can add it to your home screen like an app. If your data is slow, the video quality adjusts by itself, and text chat works on almost any connection.

Questions

Is random video chat on OhMyGoal free?

Yes. Video, voice and text chat, interests, games and every safety tool are free, and you do not need an account. Premium is an optional pass for extras such as choosing who you meet by gender or country.

Do I need to sign up?

No. You can start chatting straight away. A Google sign-in is only needed for friends and Premium.

Are chats recorded?

No. Video and messages go directly between you and the other person and are not stored by us. The other person’s device could still record their screen, so never share anything you would regret.

Who can use it?

Adults only. Everyone must be 18 or older, and the age check runs on your device before your first chat.
