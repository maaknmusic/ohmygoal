Privacy Policy

  • Random chats are peer-to-peer. Messages, voice and video are not stored on our servers. Matching uses temporary records (queue and connection-setup data) that are deleted after the chat.
  • Your IP address stays hidden from strangers. By default your connection is routed through a relay server (Metered) so the person you chat with never sees your IP address. The relay carries encrypted traffic and does not store it. You can switch this off in the menu (“Hide my IP address”); with it off, the other person’s device can see your IP address and the approximate place it points to. Games, reactions and messages travel over the same private connection.
  • Approximate distance (optional, off by default). If you turn on “Show how far I am”, your device reads its location once, rounds it to a grid square of about 5 km, and sends only that square to the person you are chatting with, and only if they also turned it on. We never receive, store or log your location. The other person sees a rough distance (for example “about 12 km away”), never an exact one. Location is never used for matching.
  • Likes. When you give someone a “good vibe” after a chat, we store a like count against their anonymous account ID, and a record that you liked that chat (your account ID, theirs and the chat’s ID) so likes cannot be repeated or faked. Likes carry no names or messages. Counts are visible to people they chat with.
  • Safety checks (age estimation, nudity detection) run on your device. Camera frames are not uploaded.
  • We store: an anonymous account ID; your chosen nickname, interests, country (detected from your IP address) and declared gender while you are matching; abuse reports; and, if you sign in with Google, your Google account ID, name and email, your friends list and friend messages, and your Premium status.
  • Abuse prevention: to enforce age and safety rules we store your IP address and a device identifier (a one-way hash of your browser and device characteristics, not a photo or hardware ID) with your account ID. If the age or nudity check results in a restriction, these are used to apply it to this device, network and account, including private windows. Restrictions expire automatically (usually after 15 minutes or 24 hours) and you can request a review from the restriction screen. If you request a review, we keep your message and any contact details you give for 90 days. The IP address and device identifier stored with your account are deleted 90 days after your last visit, and records of expired restrictions are removed 30 days after they end.
  • Payments are processed by Razorpay. We never see or store your card, UPI or bank details; we keep the order and payment IDs, amount and date.
  • Service providers: Google Firebase (database, sign-in and functions), Razorpay (payments), Metered (connection relay), Google Fonts and jsDelivr (fonts and the on-device safety models, which see your IP address when your browser downloads them), country.is (looks up your country from your IP address).
  • Your choices: you can use the Service without signing in, remove friends at any time, and ask us to delete your account data by contacting us.
