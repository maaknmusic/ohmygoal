Questions

Is OhMyGoal an Indian website?

Yes. OhMyGoal is run from India, prices are in rupees and payments are by UPI.

Can I chat in Hindi or my regional language?

Yes. Switch the language from the top of the page. The interface is available in 9 Indian languages and English, and you can add your language as an interest to meet people who speak it.

How much does Premium cost?

₹49 for 7 days, ₹149 for 30 days or ₹349 for 90 days, paid once by UPI. It does not renew by itself. Chatting itself is free.

Where do I report online blackmail in India?

Call 1930 (National Cyber Crime Helpline) or file a complaint at cybercrime.gov.in. Also report the person inside the chat.
