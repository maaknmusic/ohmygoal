Practice English speaking online

The fastest way to get better at spoken English is to speak it. The language exchange room on OhMyGoal matches you with someone who speaks English and wants to practise your language in return.

How to set it up

  1. Open the rooms and pick Language exchange.
  2. Choose the language you speak and the one you want to practise (English).
  3. Start a Voice chat. You will be matched with someone who fits.

Tips for a good practice chat

  • Agree to switch: ten minutes in English, ten in their language.
  • Ask them to correct one mistake at a time, not every word.
  • Talk about something you know well: your city, your food, cricket, films.
  • With Premium, Live translate helps when you are really stuck.

Questions

Is English speaking practice free?

Yes. The language exchange room and all chats are free.

Who will I talk to?

Adults who speak the language you are learning and want to learn yours.

Do I need to show my face?

No. Choose Voice for audio only.
