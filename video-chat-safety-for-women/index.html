Video chat safety for women

You should be able to meet new people without putting up with harassment. These are the tools OhMyGoal gives you, and practical tips that work on any site.

Tools you can use

  • Blur and masks: free background blur and a mask effect, so you share only what you want.
  • Voice or Text: start without video and switch only if you feel comfortable.
  • Gender and country filters with the Premium pass.
  • Block: a blocked person is never matched with you again. Report: moderators review it, and bans cover device, network and account.
  • Scam shield: a warning when someone shares a phone number, UPI ID or social handle.

Practical tips

  • Keep your face out of frame at first if you prefer; a blurred background hides your room.
  • Do not share your surname, workplace, college, address or social media.
  • If someone harasses or threatens you, block and report. In India call 1930 or use cybercrime.gov.in, and the women helpline 181.

Questions

Can I talk only to women?

With Premium you can choose to meet women (or men). Chatting itself is free.

What happens when I report someone?

The report goes to moderators. People who break the rules are banned by device, network and account.

Can someone record me?

We never record chats, but the other person could record their screen. Use blur, masks or voice-only if you want to stay private.
