Why Omegle closed

Omegle’s founder said that running the site had become impossible because of misuse, including adults targeting minors, and the cost of fighting it. It had very little moderation and no age check. That is exactly what we designed OhMyGoal to avoid.

OhMyGoal compared with Omegle

  • Age: Omegle asked you to tick a box. OhMyGoal estimates age on your device before your first chat and can re-check during video calls.
  • Nudity: Omegle showed whatever the camera sent. OhMyGoal blurs explicit video on your device and lets you report it in one tap.
  • Scams: a scam shield warns you when someone shares a phone number, UPI ID or social handle.
  • Bans that stick: rule-breakers are banned by device, network and account, and the ban screen says why. Appeals are free.
  • Privacy: video is peer-to-peer and never recorded. Messages are not stored.
  • More to do: interests, themed rooms, quick games, reactions, group rooms and friends.

Other things people look for after Omegle

Text-only chat (choose Text), voice without a camera (Voice), meeting people from India in your own language (video chat India), and group rooms with up to 8 people.

Questions

Is Omegle coming back?

Omegle closed for good in November 2023 and its website now only shows the founder’s farewell message. Sites using the Omegle name today are not the original Omegle.

Is OhMyGoal free like Omegle?

Yes. Random video, voice and text chat are free, with no account needed. An optional Premium pass adds extras such as gender and country filters.

Is OhMyGoal safer than Omegle was?

It is built to be: adults only with an on-device age check, nudity blurring, a scam shield, one-tap reporting and blocking, and bans that apply to the device and network, not just one account.

Does it work on phones?

Yes, in any modern browser on Android and iPhone. No app download is needed.
