How it works

  1. Choose Text on the start screen and confirm you are 18+.
  2. Add interests if you like. We look for someone who shares them.
  3. Chat, send stickers, start a game. Press Next for someone new.

Why people prefer text

  • You stay anonymous: no face, no voice.
  • You can think before you answer, which is easier in a second language.
  • With the Premium pass, Live translate lets you chat with someone who writes in another language.

Keep it friendly and safe

The scam shield warns you before you send, or when someone sends, a phone number, UPI ID or social handle. Report and Block are always one tap away.

Questions

Is text chat anonymous?

You choose a nickname; your name, number and email are never shown. Messages are not stored by us.

Do I need a camera for text chat?

No. Text chat needs no camera or microphone. Like every chat on OhMyGoal it is for adults only.

Can I switch to video later?

Yes. Go back to the start screen and choose Video or Voice for your next chat.
