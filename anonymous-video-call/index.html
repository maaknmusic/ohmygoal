Ways to stay anonymous

  • Keep the random nickname, or pick your own that does not include your real name.
  • Use the free background blur so your room cannot be recognised, or a mask effect.
  • Choose Voice or Text instead of Video.
  • With Premium, “Hide my IP address” sends the call through a relay so the other person never sees your IP address.

What anonymity does not protect

The other person can still record their own screen. So never show your face if you want to stay unknown, never share your phone number, social media or UPI ID, and never undress on camera. Our safety guide explains why.

Questions

Can strangers see my name or number?

No. They only see the nickname and avatar you choose.

Can the other person see my IP address?

A direct video call can reveal an IP address to the other side, as on any video call. With Premium, “Hide my IP address” routes the call through a relay so they cannot.

Are anonymous calls recorded?

Not by us. Calls go directly between you and the other person and are never stored.
