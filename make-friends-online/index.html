Make friends online, safely

New city, new college, or just want new people to talk to? Random chat is a quick way to meet people who like the same things. Here is how to turn a good chat into a friendship, safely.

Five steps from stranger to friend

  1. Add two or three interests so you are matched with people who share them.
  2. Use an icebreaker or a quick game to get past “hi”.
  3. Talk for a while before sharing anything personal. A city is enough; no address, school or workplace.
  4. If you both enjoyed it, add each other as friends (with a Google sign-in). Nobody can add you without your OK.
  5. Keep chatting on OhMyGoal for a while before moving to other apps.

Red flags

  • Asking for money, a UPI payment or “help” early on.
  • Pushing you to move to WhatsApp, Telegram or Instagram straight away.
  • Asking you to undress or show more on camera. Leave, block and report.
