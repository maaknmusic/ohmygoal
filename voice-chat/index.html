One-to-one voice chat

Choose Voice on the start screen. You will be matched with someone who is also looking for a voice chat. You can mute yourself, send reactions and play games during the call.

Group voice rooms

Prefer a crowd? Join a voice room on a topic you like. Rooms hold up to 8 people, and you can leave any time. Group rooms are in beta, so tell us what you would like next.

Practise a language by voice

The language exchange room matches you with someone who speaks the language you are learning and wants to learn yours. Speaking is the fastest way to improve.

Questions

Is voice chat free?

Yes, one-to-one voice chat and group voice rooms are free.

Can the other person see me?

No. In voice chat only audio is sent; your camera is not used.

Can I report someone in a voice chat?

Yes. Report and Block are available in every chat and every room.
